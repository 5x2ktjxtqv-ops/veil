# Veil Icon

Veil's icon follows the Phase 1 HUD direction: quiet, matte, native, and infrastructural.

The revised mark avoids illustrative glow and synthetic "AI poster" details. It keeps the icon close to the product surface and leans into a simple, bright utility-app silhouette:

- a bright neutral macOS-style utility tile
- a black notch capsule, echoing the always-on HUD surface
- two short status bars, reduced from the live HUD language
- one subtle horizon line for structure at large sizes

Source:

- `VeilIcon.svg` is the editable vector source.
- `Tools/render-veil-icon.swift` renders the PNG iconset and `.icns` bundle asset.
